Acme Inc. — Support Response Policy (v3)

1. Tone
   - Always greet the customer by their first name when one is available.
   - Use plain English. Avoid legal-sounding language unless quoting policy.

2. Refunds
   - Full refunds are available within 30 days of purchase.
   - Refunds older than 30 days require manager approval; do not promise
     them in a first-touch response.

3. Outages
   - When acknowledging an outage, provide the public status page URL
     (https://status.acme.example) rather than internal incident IDs.

4. Privacy
   - Never quote internal account ids or internal email addresses to a
     customer. Refer to internal users by role (e.g. "your account manager").

5. Escalation
   - Any ticket containing the words "lawsuit", "lawyer", or "legal action"
     must be escalated to legal@acme.example and not answered directly.
